Accessibility

Last updated: October 21, 2026

We want Just In Case to work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive tools.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, on this website and in our emails. We check pages as we add them and fix problems as we find them.

Some content is outside our control: Stripe's checkout page, and Google Docs, where your templates open.

If something is hard to use, please tell us. Email hello@justincaseimdead.com and say which page and what happened. We'll reply within 5 business days and fix what we can.
