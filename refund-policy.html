Refund Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

We offer subscription access to the Premium Plan through Stripe. This Refund Policy explains when refunds may or may not apply.

General rule

Refunds: once a billing period has started, it isn’t refunded, except for billing errors (like a duplicate charge) or where the law requires a refund. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Price changes: if we ever change the price, we’ll email you at least 30 days before the new price applies. You can cancel before then and won’t be charged the new price.

Canceling your subscription

You can cancel anytime.

To cancel, use the Cancel or manage subscription link at the bottom of any of our emails, or go straight to your billing page. Enter the email you signed up with and we’ll send you a sign-in link.

Charging errors

If we charge you in error, such as a duplicate charge or incorrect amount, contact us at hello@justincaseimdead.com and we will correct it.

If we discontinue the Premium Plan or a core feature

If we discontinue the Premium Plan or a core feature you are paying for, we will refund the unused, prepaid portion of your current billing period.

How to request a correction

If you believe there was an incorrect charge, email us at hello@justincaseimdead.com and include your name, the billing date, and the issue you noticed.
