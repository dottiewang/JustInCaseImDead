Refund Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

We offer subscription access to the Premium Plan through Stripe. This Refund Policy explains when refunds may or may not apply.

General rule

No refunds are issued once a billing period has started. This includes change-of-mind cancellations after the subscription has already begun.

Canceling your subscription

You can cancel anytime. Canceling stops future renewals, but it does not refund the current billing period.

Charging errors

If we charge you in error, such as a duplicate charge or incorrect amount, contact us at hello@justincaseimdead.com · Share feedback and we will correct it.

If we discontinue the Premium Plan or a core feature

If we discontinue the Premium Plan or a core feature you are paying for, we will refund the unused, prepaid portion of your current billing period.

How to request a correction

If you believe there was an incorrect charge, email us at hello@justincaseimdead.com · Share feedback and include your name, the billing date, and the issue you noticed.
