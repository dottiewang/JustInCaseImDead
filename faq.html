Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Premium Plan include?

The Premium Plan includes check-in reminders, secure document storage, an AI assistant to help you update your information, trusted contact support, and a monthly newsletter.

How much does it cost?

The Premium Plan is billed quarterly for $29.99 or annually for $100, through Stripe.

Do I need a Google account to sign up?

Yes. To sign up, you must have a personal Google account or be willing to create one before access is granted.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Subscriptions auto-renew until you cancel. You'll see a clear reminder of this at checkout.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel anytime to stop future renewals. Your current billing period is not refunded once it has started.

Are there refunds?

No refunds are issued for change-of-mind cancellations after a billing period has started. If we charge you in error, contact us and we'll correct it.

What if the Premium Plan is discontinued or a core feature is removed?

If we discontinue the Premium Plan or a core feature you're paying for, we'll refund the unused, prepaid portion of your current billing period.

Are the templates and AI assistant a substitute for legal or financial advice?

No. They are planning tools, not a replacement for a lawyer, financial advisor, or accountant. Please talk to a professional before making legal, financial, or medical decisions.
