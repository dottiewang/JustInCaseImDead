Questions

Do I need a Google account?

Yes, a free one. Use a personal account, not a work or school one — those can be closed when you change jobs, and your employer can see what's in them. No account yet? Create one here. You can sign up with the email address you already use; you don't need Gmail. Then turn on 2-Step Verification.

What if I fall behind?

No problem. Every email has a “Not this week” button that sends the same step again in 7 days. If three weeks go by without a click, we’ll pause your emails and check in with you first. And you can always come back to this page and start from any step.

Can I do it all at once?

Yes. All 9 templates are listed at the bottom of your welcome email — copy them all at once, then work straight through. When you’re done, click “Skip to the final steps” in any email and we’ll send you the print step next.

Who can see my plan?

Only you, and anyone you choose to share it with from Google Drive. Your copy lives in your account; we have no access to it.

What happens once my plan is set up?

While you’re on the premium plan, we’ll check in twice a year with a short review, so your plan stays current. Prefer a hand? You can get each checkpoint as a few 2-minute questions instead. Rather just once a year? You can switch any time.

What happens if I cancel?

The emails and checkpoints stop, but your templates stay in your Google Drive. They’re yours to keep — and if you come back later, you can pick up right where you left off.
