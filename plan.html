Questions

What if I fall behind?

Nothing happens. Every email has a “Not this week” button that pauses for 7 days, and you can always come back to this page and start from any step.

Can I do it all at once?

Yes. Open your plan and work straight through. When you're done, click “Done” in any email and we'll skip ahead.

Who can see my plan?

Only you, and anyone you choose to share it with from Google Drive. Your copy lives in your account; we have no access to it.

I don't use Google. Can I still do this?

Yes. Print the worksheets, fill them in by hand, and store them the same way as step 7.

What happens if I cancel?

The emails stop, but your plan stays in your Google Drive. It's yours to keep.
