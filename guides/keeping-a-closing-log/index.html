Closing accounts

Keeping a closing log

One page that saves dozens of repeat phone calls.

Closing accounts after a death means a lot of calls, forms and follow-ups, often spread over months. A simple log of every contact helps keep track of what's done, what's waiting, and what was promised.

What a log usually includes

ColumnExample
CompanyChase checking
Account (last 4)…4821
Date contactedOct 14
HowPhone, branch, online form
Who you spoke toMaria, bereavement team
Reference numberCB-20391
What they needCertified death certificate, letters testamentary
StatusWaiting on check to estate account

Ways families keep it

  • A notebook kept by the phone
  • A spreadsheet that others in the family can see
  • A page in the binder, next to the account list

Why it helps

  • It avoids sending the same documents twice
  • It's easy to hand off if someone else takes over
  • It shows the executor's work if beneficiaries or a court ask
  • It makes it clear when something has stalled and needs a follow-up
Good to know

Writing the date and the name of the person spoken to on every call is the single most useful habit. Many companies can look up a call by those two details.
