Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Just In Case (justincaseimdead.com) takes your privacy seriously, especially since we help you organize sensitive personal, legal, and financial information. Here's what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • If you just download a free template: nothing, unless you sign up for our newsletter.
  • If you subscribe: your name, email, and billing info (handled by Stripe — we never see your full card number).
  • Google account requirement: You must have a personal Google account to sign up for the Premium Plan. If you do not have one, you must create one in order for access to be granted.
  • Trusted contacts / family members: their name and contact info, if you choose to add them.
  • Basic site data: things like your browser and IP address, collected automatically like on most websites.

How we use it

  • To run the service — send check-in reminders, power the AI assistant
  • To bill you for your subscription
  • To communicate with your trusted contacts or family members, but only when and how you've authorized
  • To send our newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime)

We never sell your personal information.

The AI assistant

When you use our AI assistant, relevant info you've submitted may be shared with our AI provider to generate suggestions or help fill out forms. Review anything it produces before relying on it.

Who else sees your data

  • Stripe — handles payments
  • Google Forms — used to collect your submitted info
  • Amazon — only if you click an affiliate link; they don't get your documents
  • Any optional shipping vendor, only if you order a physical flash drive or binder service

We don't share your stored documents with anyone else unless you ask us to (like releasing info to a trusted contact) or we're legally required to.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no online storage is 100% secure. Keep your own backups of anything critical.

Who this service is for

We currently only offer the Services to US residents. If that changes, we'll update this policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data anytime by emailing hello@justincaseimdead.com. If you're a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA — reach out and we'll help.

Kids

This service is for adults. It's not meant for submitting a minor's personal information.

Cookies

We may use basic cookies to run the site. You can turn these off in your browser settings.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and update the date at the top.
